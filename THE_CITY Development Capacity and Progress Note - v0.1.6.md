## Current position

THE_CITY has not proven a production 10 × 10 km game. It has proven a bounded
authority machine: canonical city state owns strategic truth; local FPS work
materializes it and may submit physical evidence; canonical transactions alone
admit, revalidate, mutate, and ledger durable state.

```text
canonical causal city
        ↓
authoritative record
        ↓
Unreal first-person materialization
        ↓
physical evidence proposal
        ↓
canonical validation and atomic mutation
        ↓
new authoritative record
        ↓
fresh materialization
```

The newest proof adds a bounded external-input law: a valid crew-originated
evidence input can become a canonical boundary inside an interval that
boundary-jump execution would otherwise skip.

## Not yet capacity

- live input transport, wall-clock/active-world synchronization, same-time input ordering, late evidence, retries, malformed-input consumption, or multiple input streams;
- Causal-LOD equivalence beyond the sealed neutral fixtures: stochastic draws, actual FPS/materialization fidelity changes, repeated city-scale promotion/demotion, external inputs beyond this one interval, and richer commitment populations;
- production 10 × 10 km streaming, performance, population, or long-horizon stability;
- multiple active crews, split fireteams, simultaneous crew bubbles, or 1–4 network arbitration;
- trusted transport, rollback, save/load generalization, reconciliation, or host migration;
- intelligence quality, stale reports, UI legibility, pacing, balancing, or proof that pressure is fun at production density;
- generalized factions, commitment classes, damage, repair, economy, civilians, traffic, or city-scale procedural content.

## Development rule

The next step is not implicit expansion. The record proves one external input
inside one otherwise empty interval. Any successor must select one new risk,
freeze its authority and failure boundaries, and demonstrate it separately.
