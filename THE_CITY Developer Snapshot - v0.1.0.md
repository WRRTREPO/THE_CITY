## Current position

THE_CITY now has a bounded, mechanically evidenced causal-city machine. It is
not yet a production city, but the repository has proven that canonical truth
can persist, progress, interact with physical Unreal worlds, arbitrate multiple
physical evidence sources, own spatial identity, and change canonical
occupancy without representation becoming authority.

The newest closed dependency is:

```text
CANONICAL SITE B
      ↓ record-bound start
IN TRANSITION + EXACT RESERVATION
      ↓ publish successor
      ↓ invalidate predecessor authority
      ↓ rediscover completion from successor
CANONICAL SITE A + RESERVATION RELEASED
```

Navigation, interpolation, Unreal Actors, route geometry, cached itineraries,
historical schedule copies, and the originating start transaction do not own
location or completion.

## Proven machine

The sealed proof chain establishes these bounded capabilities:

1. Deterministic canonical causal history with inspectable provenance and
   terminal resource disposition.
2. Canonical records can materialize as distinct UE 5.8 physical worlds
   without rerolling strategic truth.
3. Real Unreal physical consequences can emit evidence, cross canonical
   validation, mutate durable truth, and survive fresh rematerialization.
4. Canonical contention, deployment opportunity cost, live arrival into an
   active commitment, and historical finality.
5. Independently defined commitments can interfere through shared canonical
   state without authored pair knowledge.
6. Bounded agent selection can propose actions without gaining transaction
   authority.
7. Resolution-local detail may change while canonical consequential truth
   remains invariant in the sealed Causal-LOD fixtures.
8. Consequential boundaries are rediscovered from each committed record;
   there is no authoritative precomputed future itinerary.
9. Player-originated evidence can enter an interval that boundary-jump
   execution would otherwise skip.
10. Same-clock successor work can continue at a later canonical phase under
    finite anti-cycle authority without an artificial clock advance.
11. A physical Unreal world can be destroyed, canonical causality can continue
    without it, and a fresh isolated Unreal process can reconstruct the evolved
    truth from the final record alone.
12. Two isolated Unreal evidence sources can enter one deterministic canonical
    external batch without emission, filesystem, presentation, or process order
    acquiring authority.
13. Two sites and one route can own canonical topology identity independently
    of conceptual labels, Unreal Actors, navigation, levels, or streaming
    representation.
14. One subject can transition between those canonical sites over canonical
    time through one explicit reservation-owning commitment.

## Authority model

```text
CONCEPTUAL REFERENCE
    informs specification only
            ↓
CANONICAL RECORD
    owns strategic facts, identities, schedules, commitments,
    reservations, dispositions, ancestry, and causal provenance
            ↓
RECORD-BOUND DISCOVERY / ADMISSION
            ↓
ONE DECLARED CANONICAL RESOLUTION PATH
            ↓
ATOMIC SUCCESSOR RECORD
            ↓
PREDECESSOR CAPABILITIES LOSE AUTHORITY
            ↓
REDISCOVER FROM THE SUCCESSOR

UNREAL / LOCAL REPRESENTATION
    verifies and materializes canonical truth
    may produce non-authoritative physical evidence
    never owns canonical mutation, chronology, arbitration, or topology
```

The shortest governing rule is:

> **Canonical records own truth. Physical worlds express truth and may provide
> evidence. Only canonical admission and resolution change the city.**

## Not yet proven

The current corpus does not establish:

- physical traversal, navigation, interpolation, animation, coordinates,
  distance, speed, or production travel time;
- route occupancy, capacity, traffic, congestion, collision, interruption,
  rerouting, recovery, or mid-transition topology mutation;
- multiple subjects, split crews, simultaneous physical domains, concurrent
  occupancy, or site/route contention;
- live network transport, multiplayer arbitration, rollback, reconciliation,
  save/load, host migration, or shared multi-owner persistence;
- proximity-driven promotion, World Partition, production streaming, city
  population, traffic simulation, or city-scale performance;
- stochastic identity, generalized planning, production topology, or a
  generalized movement/occupancy system.
