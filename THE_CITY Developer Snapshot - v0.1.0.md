## Authority model

```text
CONCEPTUAL REFERENCE
    informs specification only
            ↓
CANONICAL RECORD
    owns strategic facts, identities, schedules, commitments,
    reservations, dispositions, ancestry, and causal provenance
            ↓
RECORD-BOUND DISCOVERY / ADMISSION
            ↓
ONE DECLARED CANONICAL RESOLUTION PATH
            ↓
ATOMIC SUCCESSOR RECORD
            ↓
PREDECESSOR CAPABILITIES LOSE AUTHORITY
            ↓
REDISCOVER FROM THE SUCCESSOR

UNREAL / LOCAL REPRESENTATION
    verifies and materializes canonical truth
    may produce non-authoritative physical evidence
    never owns canonical mutation, chronology, arbitration, or topology
```

The shortest governing rule is:

> **Canonical records own truth. Physical worlds express truth and may provide
> evidence. Only canonical admission and resolution change the city.**
